# Guild Marketing OS Customer Research Method

Status: live private

Use when a Guild Marketing OS agent needs to turn interviews, sales calls, surveys, support tickets, reviews, community threads, or public discussion into reusable audience, ICP, messaging, content, or campaign inputs.

## Method

1. Identify the research mode: existing source analysis, public-source discovery, or a combined pass.
2. Confirm the audience, segment, time period, source owner, and approval status.
3. Extract jobs to be done, struggling moments, pains, trigger events, desired outcomes, objections, alternatives, and exact audience language.
4. Keep verbatim language separate from interpretation.
5. Cluster findings by theme, then score each theme by frequency, intensity, source quality, recency, segment fit, and contradiction risk.
6. Label every insight as approved evidence, user-supplied evidence, source claim, inference, assumption, or missing evidence.
7. Route approved themes into audience, positioning, messaging, content, campaign, AEO, and proof artifacts.

## Source Quality Rules

- Do not build personas or message strategy from fewer than five independent data points per segment unless the output is clearly labeled hypothesis.
- Weight sources from the last 12 months higher than older sources unless the project leader approves a longer window.
- Treat support tickets as problem-heavy, review sites as opinion-heavy, and public forums as community-specific rather than market-representative.
- Treat company-authored pages as useful claims, not independent validation.
- Flag contradictions between what users say, what they do, and what performance data shows.
- Do not quote private interviews, tickets, or calls in public copy unless the source owner approves reuse.

## Output Standard

Every customer research packet should include:

- Source set and coverage gaps.
- Top themes ranked by frequency and intensity.
- Verbatim language bank with source labels.
- JTBD, struggling moments, pains, triggers, desired outcomes, objections, and alternatives.
- Fit, non-fit, status quo, and switching-anxiety signals.
- Segment-specific differences.
- Confidence label for each insight.
- Implications for ICP, positioning, messaging, content, campaigns, and AEO.
- Approval decisions required before reuse.

## Handoff Targets

- Company Context Builder receives approved audience facts, proof notes, and missing context.
- ICP receives persona, segment, trigger, objection, and fit signals.
- Messaging receives verbatim language, objections, desired outcomes, and proof needs.
- Positioning Fit And Proof receives struggling moments, candidate sets, anxieties, proof needs, and metric hypotheses.
- Market Signal receives source gaps and public-source follow-up targets.
- Campaigns And Paid Media receives audience-message fit, creative angles, and claim constraints.

## Claim Rules

Do not turn customer language into a public claim unless the claim is backed by approved evidence. If the source supports only a pain, phrase it as audience language or hypothesis, not as proof of product performance.
