# Guild Marketing OS Strategic Decision Method

Status: live private

Use when a Guild Marketing OS agent needs to synthesize approved context, market signal, customer research, competitive intelligence, performance data, or project-leader input into a higher-stakes GTM strategy recommendation.

## Method

1. Frame the decision: topic, owner, audience, time horizon, constraints, approval owner, available sources, missing sources, and what the recommendation must help decide.
2. Confirm the decision mode: positioning direction, ICP prioritization, campaign investment, category narrative, competitor response, web/AEO strategy, market entry, budget allocation, offer strategy, or another GTM tradeoff.
3. Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, unknowns, and do-not-use claims before recommending a path.
4. Compare 2 to 4 realistic options, including the status quo when it is a plausible choice.
5. Run a perspective scan with 4 to 6 relevant views, such as buyer, project leader, GTM operator, competitor, evidence reviewer, implementation owner, finance owner, brand/legal reviewer, or skeptic.
6. Map contradictions between sources, incentives, audience needs, proof, channel realities, and operational constraints.
7. Identify the decision hinge: the question or assumption that would most change the recommendation.
8. Stress-test the recommendation by naming the weakest claim, bias risk, missing perspective, counterfactual, failure signal, and stop condition.
9. Define what to verify next, what to measure after action, and what evidence would change the recommendation.

## Evidence Rules

- Label every decisive claim as approved evidence, user-supplied evidence, source claim, inference, assumption, unknown, missing evidence, or do-not-use.
- Prefer primary sources, approved context artifacts, current-session user input, and approved connected data over generic market assumptions.
- Treat company-authored material as useful context, not independent validation.
- Treat pricing, privacy, security, compliance, retention, guarantees, live execution, customer counts, audience size, performance claims, and budget recommendations as high-risk.
- If evidence is weak, source access is limited, or sources conflict, state that plainly and lower recommendation confidence.
- Do not average away contradictions. Explain which contradiction matters, why it matters, and who should resolve it.

## Output Standard

Every strategic decision packet should include:

- Decision frame: decision, owner, audience, timeframe, constraints, sources, and missing evidence.
- Recommendation: the proposed path, confidence, why it wins, and what it does not authorize.
- Options considered: realistic alternatives, upside, downside, evidence, and rejection reason.
- Perspective scan: each view's core read, supporting evidence, blind spot exposed, weakness, and confidence.
- Contradiction map: conflicts across sources, stakeholders, incentives, proof, or execution realities.
- Evidence ledger: 5 to 8 decisive claims with evidence label, confidence, and what would change each claim.
- Decision hinge: if the hinge is true, the implication; if false, the alternative implication.
- Risks and approval gates: required human decisions before public claims, spend, launch, publishing, CRM activation, or external system changes.
- Stress test: weakest claim, bias risk, missing perspective, counterfactual, stop condition, and failure signal.
- What to verify next, what to measure, and what would change the recommendation.

## Stop Conditions

Choose exactly one stop condition for the recommendation:

- Safe to use as directional input.
- Useful, but requires verification before action.
- Not safe to use yet.

## Handoff Targets

- Company Context Builder receives updated approved facts, unresolved contradictions, proof constraints, workspace-context summary candidates, and missing source requests.
- Market Signal receives source gaps, contradiction targets, peer/competitor follow-up, and watchlist signals.
- ICP receives audience-priority decisions, disqualifiers, decision criteria, and confidence limits.
- Messaging receives recommended positioning direction, claim boundaries, proof gaps, objections, and answer-ready decision language.
- Branding And Pitch Deck receives strategic narrative, proof hierarchy, executive story implications, and production boundaries.
- Answer Engine And Web Readiness receives entity clarity decisions, priority query implications, proof needs, and web/AEO tradeoffs.
- Campaigns And Paid Media receives budget, offer, channel, destination, creative-angle, KPI, and approval-gate implications.

## Claim Rules

Do not present a strategic recommendation as authorization to publish, spend, launch, activate audiences, change CRM data, update websites, configure credentials, or change external systems. Keep every recommendation reviewable, evidence-labeled, and gated by the right human owner.
