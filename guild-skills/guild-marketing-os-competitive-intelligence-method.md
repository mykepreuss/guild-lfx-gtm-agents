# Guild Marketing OS Competitive Intelligence Method

Status: live private

Use when a Guild Marketing OS agent needs to research competitors, peers, alternatives, category language, comparison pages, battlecard inputs, market positioning, or competitor-driven content opportunities.

## Method

1. Confirm the comparison set: direct competitors, adjacent alternatives, status quo, internal build, agencies, or community-led alternatives.
2. Confirm the intended use: market signal, positioning, ICP, sales enablement, content, campaign planning, or AEO.
3. Gather source-approved evidence from supplied URLs, public pages, reviews, search data, user-provided notes, analyst/media sources, or connected tools when approved.
4. Preserve raw source labels and dates before synthesizing.
5. Extract positioning, audience, features, pricing claims, proof, customers, integrations, categories, content themes, review themes, and recent product signals.
6. Score each finding by source quality, freshness, audience relevance, contradiction risk, and usefulness for downstream decisions.
7. Produce honest comparisons that name competitor strengths, customer fit, and unknowns.

## Evidence Rules

- Make every competitor claim traceable to a source label or mark it as inference.
- Treat pricing, customer counts, security, compliance, retention, guarantees, and performance claims as high-risk.
- Do not exaggerate weaknesses or declare a winner unless the comparison criteria and evidence support it.
- Mark stale competitor pages, unavailable pricing, missing changelog data, and review-sample bias.
- Keep competitor-owned claims separate from third-party review or customer evidence.

## Output Standard

Every competitive intelligence packet should include:

- Competitor or alternative set.
- Source list with dates and coverage gaps.
- At-a-glance comparison table.
- Positioning and message patterns.
- Audience and use-case fit.
- Pricing and packaging signals with confidence labels.
- Product, feature, integration, and content signals.
- Review, community, or customer-language themes when supplied.
- Opportunities for messaging, AEO, content, sales enablement, and campaigns.
- Claims that require legal, brand, product, or project-leader approval.

## Handoff Targets

- Market Signal receives category, peer, search, review, and community signals.
- Messaging receives differentiation, objections, alternatives, and no-go claims.
- ICP receives audience-fit and anti-fit patterns.
- Campaigns And Paid Media receives comparison angles, destination needs, and proof gaps.
- Branding And Pitch Deck receives narrative, pitch, and market-shift context.
- Sales Enablement future work receives battlecard and objection inputs.

## Claim Rules

Do not publish competitor comparisons, alternative pages, battlecards, or ads without approval of factual claims, trademark usage, screenshots, pricing references, and legal-sensitive positioning.
