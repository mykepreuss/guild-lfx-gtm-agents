# Guild Marketing OS Conversion Experimentation Method

Status: live private

Use when a Guild Marketing OS agent needs to review conversion paths, landing pages, forms, signup flows, campaign destinations, tracking plans, KPIs, A/B tests, or performance loops.

## Method

1. Confirm the page, flow, audience, traffic source, conversion goal, baseline data, and decision the project leader needs to make.
2. Audit the experience for value clarity, message match, CTA hierarchy, proof placement, objection handling, friction, accessibility, mobile usability, and next-step clarity.
3. Separate issues that can be fixed from hypotheses that require testing.
4. Create hypotheses in this form: because evidence shows X, we believe change Y will improve metric Z for audience A, while guardrail G should not worsen.
5. Define the primary metric, secondary metrics, guardrails, sample-size needs, run window, and decision rule before interpreting results.
6. Create or review a tracking plan that captures the minimum events and properties needed to make the decision.
7. Route implementation, analytics, brand, legal, privacy, and live-experiment approvals to the right owner.

## Review Dimensions

- Value proposition clarity: the user can tell what this is, who it is for, and why it matters quickly.
- Message match: the page matches the source promise from search, social, email, ad, event, or referral.
- CTA design: one primary action, clear label, repeated at natural decision points, and supported by secondary paths only when useful.
- Proof and trust: claims are near relevant CTAs and backed by approved evidence.
- Objection handling: price, fit, switching, implementation, community, risk, and support questions are addressed.
- Friction: form length, required fields, page speed, mobile layout, navigation, and unclear post-submit expectations.
- Measurement: events, UTMs, conversion definitions, attribution assumptions, and dashboard fields are documented.

## Output Standard

Every conversion or experimentation packet should include:

- Consumed context and current evidence.
- Conversion goal and decision to be made.
- Quick fixes that do not require experimentation.
- High-impact recommendations with evidence labels.
- Test backlog with hypotheses, effort, expected impact, and confidence.
- Tracking plan and event/property definitions.
- Primary, secondary, and guardrail metrics.
- Sample-size or traffic sufficiency note.
- Approval gates before publishing, testing, tagging, or changing live systems.

## Handoff Targets

- Campaigns And Paid Media receives landing-page, KPI, and performance-loop recommendations.
- Messaging receives message-match and proof-language gaps.
- Branding And Pitch Deck receives page-structure and web-experience direction.
- Dashboard Signals receives KPI, event, guardrail, and approval-state definitions.
- Channel Registry receives destination, tracking, and live-activation blockers.

## Claim Rules

Do not claim conversion improvement, statistical significance, revenue lift, or test results without approved performance data and a clear methodology. Do not recommend spend changes from weak or incomplete tracking.
