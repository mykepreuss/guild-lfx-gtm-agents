# Guild Marketing OS Campaign Planning And Creative Testing Method

Status: live private

Use when a Guild Marketing OS agent needs to plan campaigns, paid media, content promotion, event promotion, creative angles, channel tests, budget assumptions, landing-page needs, or performance review loops.

## Method

1. Confirm objective, audience, offer, channel, destination, budget range, timing, KPI, proof constraints, and approval owner.
2. Match campaign type to intent: awareness, education, community engagement, lead capture, event registration, trial/demo request, re-engagement, sponsor/supporter growth, or paid acquisition.
3. Map each segment to pain, desired outcome, claim, proof, channel fit, creative angle, destination, and suppression rule.
4. Draft a campaign structure with naming conventions, variants, source tags, reporting fields, and approval states.
5. Build creative tests around distinct angles before testing minor wording changes.
6. Define the performance loop: what to review, how often, what signal is sufficient, and what decisions are allowed.
7. Keep live spend, audience sync, publishing, scheduling, and account changes behind explicit approval.

## Planning Checks

- Audience: approved segment, inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, consent, frequency guardrails, and data owner.
- Offer: approved value proposition, proof, CTA, urgency or event timing, and no-go claims.
- Channel: owned, rented, borrowed, paid, partner, community, event, or lifecycle channel.
- Destination: landing page, docs page, form, event page, newsletter, community action, or sales handoff.
- Creative: angle, hook, format, platform limits, proof, CTA, and brand constraints.
- Measurement: UTM/source tags, conversion event, reporting owner, dashboard field, target, and guardrail.
- Approval: project leader, marketing owner, brand, legal, privacy, data, finance, or platform owner.

## Output Standard

Every campaign planning packet should include:

- Campaign brief.
- Audience-message matrix.
- Channel and destination plan.
- Creative angle set with variants.
- Test plan and decision rules.
- Tracking, UTM, and dashboard requirements.
- Budget assumptions and what they do or do not authorize.
- Launch, spend, audience, publishing, and optimization gates.
- Performance review loop with pause, revise, scale, or continue criteria.

## Handoff Targets

- Campaigns And Paid Media receives the full campaign packet.
- Social Monitoring And Content receives platform-safe post, reply, and content variants.
- Messaging receives claim, CTA, and proof gaps.
- Audience Segmentation receives inclusion, exclusion, consent, and suppression requirements.
- Dashboard Signals receives KPI, guardrail, review cadence, and decision-state fields.
- Channel Registry receives channel status, destination, owner, and blocked live actions.

## Claim Rules

Do not imply ads, posts, emails, audiences, events, or campaigns were launched, scheduled, synced, or optimized unless an approved live action confirms it. Recommendations may say draft, plan, propose, test, review, pause, revise, or route for approval.
