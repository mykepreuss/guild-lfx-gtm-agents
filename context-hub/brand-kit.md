## Visual Direction

- Technical.
- Dependable.
- Community-led.

## Starter Palette Direction

- Deep green for reliability.
- Signal blue for infrastructure.
- Neutral gray for documentation.

## Typography Direction

Use a clear developer-documentation feel. Avoid playful consumer styling.

## Banner Guidance

Show system diagrams, contributor activity, and deployment pathways rather than abstract gradients.

## Voice

- Clear.
- Practical.
- Technically credible.
- Community-respectful.
