# Start Here: Guild Marketing OS

Guild Marketing OS is a team of focused marketing agents that work together
inside one Guild workspace. You describe the marketing outcome you need;
Marketing OS chooses the right specialist and returns a draft for your review.

You do not need to know which agent to select or how the agents work behind the
scenes.

## What You Can Create

Ask in normal marketing language. For example:

- “Assess the market themes and competitor signals that should shape our plan.”
- “Define our ideal customer profile and buying committee.”
- “Create useful audience segments and channel recommendations.”
- “Develop our positioning and three message pillars.”
- “Create a presentation for our new product launch.”
- “Build a four-week organic content plan.”
- “Create an integrated campaign with channels, creative ideas, KPIs, and
  budget assumptions.”

Marketing OS creates reviewable drafts. It does not publish content, schedule
posts, spend budget, change CRM data, configure credentials, or make legal
decisions.

## Set Up Marketing OS

1. Create or select a dedicated workspace for Marketing OS.
2. Install **Marketing OS Launcher** from Guild Agent Hub.
3. In Launcher Chat, send:

   `Continue Marketing OS onboarding`

4. Approve the installation requests for Company Context Builder and the seven
   marketing specialists.
5. In the workspace settings, make **Marketing OS Launcher** the default agent.
6. Continue in the same Launcher Chat so your drafts and progress stay
   together.

No CLI, cloud account, database, CRM connection, advertising account, or
publishing credential is required.

## Add Your Company Context

Company context gives every specialist the same approved starting point. You
can write it conversationally or use this template:

```text
Set up Marketing OS for [company].

Company description:
[One short paragraph explaining what the company or product does.]

Primary audiences:
[The customers, buyers, or users you want to reach.]

Current marketing goal:
[The outcome you are working toward.]

Approved claims:
[Facts or proof that agents may reuse.]

Channels in scope:
[For example: website, email, organic social, events, paid media.]

Important constraints:
[Anything the agents must not claim or recommend.]

Keep everything draft-only.
```

Company Context Builder will show:

- What it understood.
- What can be reused.
- What remains unapproved or needs evidence.
- What it needs from you next.

When the essentials are present—company description, audiences, marketing
goal, channel scope, and constraints—the baseline is ready to approve. Missing
case studies, proof points, brand guidance, or other useful detail appears as
an optional improvement, not homework that prevents you from starting.

Review the summary, use the exact approval command shown by Launcher, and then
publish the compact brief to the workspace. You can add or revise company
context later as the work evolves. Marketing OS keeps artifact approval
separate from publishing that brief to the workspace.

## Request Marketing Work

After company context is ready, ask for the outcome—not an agent name:

```text
Create a launch campaign for our enterprise audience. Include the campaign
idea, messaging, channels, creative concepts, landing-page brief, budget
assumptions, KPIs, and a 90-day plan. Keep it draft-only.
```

```text
Create a 10-slide presentation for our leadership team explaining the market
opportunity, target audience, positioning, launch plan, proof, and next steps.
Include a visual direction for each slide.
```

Launcher selects the appropriate specialist, gives it the approved company
context, and returns the complete draft in the same Chat.

## How To Review A Result

Every result should make five things clear:

1. What was created.
2. Which company context and evidence it used.
3. Which assumptions or facts still need review.
4. What is safe to reuse.
5. What you should do next.

If information is missing, answer the focused question and ask Marketing OS to
continue. You should not need to paste the company context again.

## Current V1 Boundary

Marketing OS drafts and organizes marketing work for human review. It does not
execute campaigns or change external systems. When a result discusses market
or social signals, it must say whether the evidence was supplied by you,
inspected through a read-only connection, or observed through live monitoring.

That boundary keeps the suite useful without pretending that an external action
or live observation occurred.
